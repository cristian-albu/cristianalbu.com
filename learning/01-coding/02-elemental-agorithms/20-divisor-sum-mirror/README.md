# Divisor Sum Mirror

### Requirement

`n` natural numbers are given. Determine the sum of the divisors of the mirrors of the `n` numbers.

### Input data

The program reads the number `n` from the keyboard, and then `n` natural numbers, separated by spaces.

### Output data

The program will display on the screen the number `S`, representing the required sum.

### Restrictions and specifications

- 1 ≤ n ≤ 1000
- the `n` read numbers will be less than 1,000,000,000

### Example:

"Input"

```
3
12 23 30
```

"Output"

```
99
```

### Explanation

The mirror of `12` is `21`; the sum of its divisors is `32`. The mirror of `23` is `32`; the sum of its divisors is `63`. The mirror of `30` is `3`; the sum of its divisors is `4`.
